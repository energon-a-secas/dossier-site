Checkout slowdown, 8 October

Draft by D. Whitfield, on-call engineer. Not yet reviewed.

Summary

Between 14:04 and 14:51 UTC checkout was slow enough that about one attempt in sixteen timed out. The cart-api 4.18 deploy shrank the database connection pool, and client retries made it worse. We rolled back, and latency recovered seven minutes later.

Customer impact

  • 1,940 of 31,300 checkout attempts timed out (6.2%).
  • 1,712 of those customers retried and finished their order.
  • p95 latency peaked at 2.9 s against a usual 420 ms.
  • Support logged 37 contacts about slow checkout.

Root causes

The release copied a staging profile that sets the pool to 12 connections instead of 50. At peak the pool ran out in about two minutes, and each timed-out call was retried up to five times, which tripled the requests waiting for a connection.

The staging profile was never meant to ship. The deploy config review does not look at pool sizes today.

What we did

Rolled back to 4.17 at 14:44 and watched the pool drain. Latency was normal by 14:51. We kept 4.18 off the canary list until the profile was fixed.

What went well

The rollback was one command and took seven minutes. Support had a status note up within twenty minutes of the page.

Nobody was charged twice: the payment provider deduplicated every retried order.

Follow-up actions

  1. Add pool size to the deploy config review. Owner: checkout team, due 22 October.
  2. Cap client retries at two, with backoff. Owner: web team, due 29 October.
  3. Alert on pool saturation, not only on latency. Owner: SRE, due 29 October.
