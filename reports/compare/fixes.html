Incident report: checkout latency spike on 8 October

The agent's report, corrected by D. Whitfield against the metrics and the deploy log. Illustrative example, invented data.

Summary

On 8 October from 14:04 to 14:51 UTC, p95 checkout latency rose from 420 ms to 2.9 s and 6.2% of checkout attempts timed out. The trigger was a cart-api deploy that lowered the database connection pool from 50 to 12.

Root cause

The 4.18 release exhausted the primary database connection pool under peak load.

Checkout clients retry a timed-out call up to five times, which tripled the requests waiting for a connection.

Impact

  • 1,940 of 31,300 checkout attempts timed out (6.2%).
  • 1,712 of those customers retried and finished their order.
  • No data was lost.
  • Support received 37 contacts about slow checkout.

Timeline

  • 14:02: deployment of 4.18 begins.
  • 14:04: p95 latency exceeds 800 ms.
  • 14:13: latency alert pages on-call, nine minutes after the slowdown began.
  • 14:44: rollback to 4.17 begins.
  • 14:51: rollback complete, p95 back under 800 ms.

Remediation

The deployment was rolled back to 4.17 and latency returned to normal.

Action items

  • Add pool size to the deploy config review. Owner: checkout team, due 22 October.
  • Cap client retries at two, with backoff. Owner: web team, due 29 October.
  • Alert on pool saturation, not only on latency. Owner: SRE, due 29 October.
